Cross-exchange Spread (A - B)

Perp spread differential: log(ask A) − log(bid B) on exchange A minus same on exchange B. Above zero = exchange A prices the pair richer. Reverts to mean = tradeable. Funding overlay shows how much of the spread is funding cost vs true mispricing.
