Quick Start

  • Mode: Cross-Exchange — compares the perp spread (log ask A − log bid B) of two assets across all exchange pairs. Long the pair on the cheaper exchange, short on the richer one. Bets the spread converges. Includes funding + slippage costs.
  • Mode: Single-Exchange — compares all selected perp pair combinations on one exchange. Long one perp, short the other. Bets the ratio reverts to its mean. Matches the Paper Trade backtest strategy.
  • Pick a Preset — Auto (std-based) adapts entry/exit to each pair's volatility (recommended). Conservative (0.15%, few safe trades), Balanced (0.10%, default), or Aggressive (0.06%, more trades, higher risk).
  • Hours = how far back to look at spread data. Longer windows (24h+) give more trades and more reliable statistics. Shorter windows (1-4h) show recent conditions but may have too few trades to be meaningful.
  • Data source = real live perp market data collected from exchange WebSockets (Hyperliquid, Paradex, GRVT, Aevo, Asterdex, Binance). Prices are saved every second to compressed CSV files. All historical files are loaded — if the server has been running for 3 days, Hours=72 will have the full 3 days of data.
  • Entry = spread must reach this level before a trade is triggered. Higher = fewer but bigger trades. In Auto mode, this is N × std (adapts per pair).
  • Exit = spread must return below this to close the trade. Should be lower than Entry.
  • Max Std = filters out pairs with unreliable data (high spread volatility = stale prices). 0.1% is a good default. Anything above 0.5% likely means an exchange has frozen prices.
  • Margin x Leverage = your position size. $100 x 10x = $1,000 deployed per trade.
  • Execution = Aggressive (taker) fills immediately but costs more in fees. Passive (maker) is cheaper but may not fill.
  • Slippage = estimated slippage per fill as % of notional. Applied to all 4 fills (2 entry + 2 exit).
  • Funding = perp funding cost accumulated over hold time. Long pays, short receives. Scaled by 8h funding periods.
  • Look for pairs with high Total Net, high Win%, and high Sharpe. The Recommended badge marks the best overall pair.
  • Click Paper trade on any row to simulate that pair with these exact settings.
