How It Works

The bot runs a market-neutral spread strategy on BTC/HYPE perpetual futures across multiple sub-accounts. Here is the cycle, step by step.

01

Hedge Open

The bot opens 4 legs simultaneously — long BTC + short HYPE on account A, short BTC + long HYPE on account B. The positions are mirror images, so the net market exposure is zero.

02

Divergence Detection

The bot polls both accounts every 2 seconds, tracking the P&L of each side. When the spread diverges enough to cover fees and lock in profit, the winning side is closed.

03

Bank the Winner

The winning account is market-closed, locking in the spread profit. The gate then hands the turn to the next bot group so both groups alternate cycles.

04

Recovery & Re-hedge

The losing side rides back toward breakeven. If it draws down further, the bot re-opens the winning side as a recovery hedge — banking small profits to offset the loss until the original position recovers or hits the stop.

Risk Management

Every cycle has defined exit parameters. The bot never holds naked directional risk — every position is hedged.

⚖

Position Sizing

Each leg is sized at a fixed notional with 10x leverage. Margin per account is kept low relative to equity, ensuring the stop-loss triggers long before any liquidation risk.

⚠

Hard Stop-Loss

Every cycle has a maximum adverse excursion limit. If the losing side reaches the stop, the bot market-closes the entire position immediately. No averaging down, no hoping.

↻

Re-hedge Recovery

Instead of accepting the full loss, the bot re-opens the winning side when the loser draws down further. Each re-hedge banks a small profit, gradually offsetting the unrealized loss until the original position recovers.

⚙

Sequential Gating

Two bot groups (AB and CD) alternate via a gate file. Only one group opens a new cycle at a time, preventing overexposure and keeping API usage within rate limits.
