How It Works

The bot runs a market-neutral spread strategy on BTC/HYPE perpetual futures across multiple sub-accounts.

01

Hedge Open

4 legs opened simultaneously — mirror positions across two accounts. Net market exposure is zero.

02

Divergence Detection

Both accounts polled every 2 seconds. When the spread diverges enough to cover fees and profit, the winner closes.

03

Bank the Winner

Winning account market-closed, locking in spread profit. Gate hands turn to the next bot group.

04

Recovery & Re-hedge

Loser rides back toward breakeven. If it draws down further, the bot re-hedges to bank small profits offsetting the loss.

Risk Management

Every cycle has defined exit parameters. The bot never holds naked directional risk.

⚖

Position Sizing

Fixed notional per leg with 10x leverage. Margin kept low relative to equity — stop-loss triggers long before liquidation.

⚠

Hard Stop-Loss

Maximum adverse excursion limit per cycle. Position market-closed immediately at stop. No averaging down.

↻

Re-hedge Recovery

Bot re-opens winning side on further drawdown, banking small profits to offset unrealized loss until recovery.

⚙

Sequential Gating

Two bot groups alternate via a gate file. One group opens at a time — no overexposure, API rate limits respected.
